# Asset inventory

The two-building campus map keeps a deliberately small asset set.

| Asset | Source | Licence | Format | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature Kit | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | CC0 1.0 | glb | Tree meshes only |
| Trafton East floor plans (floors 1-2) | https://mankato.mnsu.edu/about-the-university/maps-and-transportation/maps-and-directions/university-area-map/building-floor-plans/ | © Minnesota State University, Mankato | pdf | Engineering Building interiors |
| Clinical Sciences floor plans (floors 1-2) | same index | © Minnesota State University, Mankato | pdf | Medical School Building interiors |
| OpenStreetMap site geometry | https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright | ODbL 1.0 | json | Footprints, road and walkway network |

The floor-plan PDFs are served unmodified from `public/assets/floorplans/`. The scene renders the drawing and derives simple raised wall runs from it; no university 3D model files are bundled.

The former modular building kits, city kits, Grab3D hall and Poly Haven textures have been removed.
